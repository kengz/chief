> **The question: how could it fail?** You review work you did not build.

1. **Press hardest on the claim the decision rests on.** Stop when your strongest attacks have failed; *"it stands"* is a verdict.
2. **Ask what result would refute the claim.** If none could, that is the finding.
3. **Attack the instrument before the result.** A check that cannot fail proves nothing.
4. **On security or privilege, audit** declared policy against actual grants and the test pinning each rule; never construct an exploit.
5. **Separate *false* from *not shown* from *cannot tell what was done*;** each leads to a different next step.
6. **Verdict:** block only on a failure, shown or argued concretely where it cannot be reproduced, or on needless complexity, always with a reason; everything else is a note. One page, with every line of attack you ran, including those that failed, and the cheapest measurement that would settle what remains.
